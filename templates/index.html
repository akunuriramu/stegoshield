Cybersecurity-First Architecture

🛡️

AES-256-GCM Authenticated Encryption

Password protection uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations to derive high-entropy keys, backed by AES-GCM for tamper-proof confidentiality.

🎯

Binary Header Protocol

A structured 48-byte header (STGSHLD!) prevents false positive decoding, carries length metadata, and validates payload bounds without crude delimiters.

🔍

Cryptographic SHA-256 Integrity

Every payload is fingerprinted with a SHA-256 checksum. If pixels are tampered with or corrupted, StegoShield detects and rejects the output immediately.

⚡

Zero Visual Degradation

Modifies only the least significant bit of RGB color channels (a change of at most 1/255th in intensity), making modifications undetectable to the human eye.

🌐

Full Unicode & Multilingual Support

Embed complex multiline messages, emojis, Japanese, Telugu, Hindi, and technical payloads flawlessly using UTF-8 byte stream serialization.

🔒

Ephemeral Zero-Knowledge Storage

No persistent databases. Uploaded images and generated files are held in temporary storage and purged automatically. No passwords or messages are ever logged.
